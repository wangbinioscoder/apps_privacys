AI形象顾问隐私政策

This page provides bilingual privacy information for AIStyleAdvisor. / 本页面提供 AI形象顾问（AIStyleAdvisor）的中英双语隐私说明。

English

Information We Process

  • Photos or images that you choose or capture, such as selfies and clothing photos, are used to provide AI style scans, smart wardrobe features, and AI try-on results.
  • Generated style reports, try-on history, favorites, nickname, and avatar may be used to provide a personalized in-app experience.
  • Subscription status is processed through Apple StoreKit to determine access to Style Pro features.

Photos and AI Services

When you actively use AI scan, AI try-on, hairstyle, or style recommendation features, the app may send the images you select to third-party AI services or object storage services to generate the result you requested. We do not use your photos for unrelated purposes.

Permission Usage

  • Camera permission is used when you choose to take selfies or clothing photos.
  • Photo library access is used when you choose photos for AI analysis or try-on.
  • Photo library write access is used only when you choose to save generated results.

Subscriptions and Payments

Style Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple App Store. We do not directly collect or store your bank card information, payment credentials, or Apple ID password.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local profile information, avatar, favorites, wardrobe items, and history are stored on your device. You can edit or delete related content in the app. Removing the app usually removes locally stored app data.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, please contact us through the support page or the App Store support channel.

中文

我们处理的数据

  • 用户主动选择或拍摄的自拍、衣物照片：用于生成形象扫描、智能衣橱和 AI 试穿效果。
  • 应用内生成的形象报告、试穿历史、收藏、昵称和头像：用于提供个性化体验。
  • 订阅状态：通过 Apple StoreKit 获取，用于判断 Style Pro 权益。

照片与 AI 服务

当用户主动使用 AI 扫描、AI 试穿、发型或形象建议功能时，应用可能会将用户选择的图片发送到第三方 AI 服务或对象存储服务进行处理，以生成用户请求的结果。我们不会将照片用于与功能无关的目的。

权限用途

  • 相机权限：用于拍摄自拍或衣物照片。
  • 相册读取权限：用于从相册选择照片进行 AI 分析或试穿。
  • 相册写入权限：仅在用户主动保存生成结果时使用。

订阅与付款

Style Pro 订阅由 Apple App Store 处理付款和订阅管理。我们不会直接收集或存储用户的银行卡、支付凭据或 Apple ID 密码。

数据保留与删除

本地昵称、头像、收藏、衣橱和历史记录保存在设备本地。用户可以在应用中修改或删除相关内容。卸载应用通常会移除本地数据。

联系我们

如有隐私相关问题，请通过技术支持页面或 App Store 支持渠道与我们联系。
