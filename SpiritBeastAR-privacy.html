Spirit Beast AR Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Introduction

Spirit Beast AR, also known as 灵镜神兽园, is an augmented reality creature collection app. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it protects your privacy.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect personal identity information, precise location, contacts, financial information, advertising identifiers, or account credentials. The app does not require account registration.

Camera and AR Usage

The app requests camera access to display AR content, scan nearby objects, and place 3D spirit beasts in the real world. Camera frames are processed on device for gameplay and are not uploaded to our servers.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app may store gameplay progress locally, including unlocked beasts, catalog progress, achievements, mirror level, and related preferences. This data stays on your device unless you choose to back up your device through Apple services.

Photos and Sharing

If you use AR photo features, generated photos are handled through iOS sharing and photo workflows controlled by you. The app does not automatically upload your photos.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking services for personal data collection.

Data Security

Because gameplay data is stored locally, protecting your device passcode and Apple ID helps protect your app data as well.

Data Deletion

You can delete local app data by deleting the app from your device. iOS may also remove app data according to system backup and storage settings.

Children's Privacy

The app is designed as a general audience AR experience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
