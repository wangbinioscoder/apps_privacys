ConeLog — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 8, 2026

1. Introduction

ConeLog ("the App", "we", "us") is a training journal for freestyle slalom skaters. We deeply respect your privacy. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the App handles and what it does not.

Bottom line: ConeLog is offline-first. It does not require an account, does not embed any third-party analytics or ad SDK, and never sells or shares your data. Optional features (iCloud sync, weather capture, location capture) are powered exclusively by Apple's first-party frameworks and only activate with your explicit consent.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

ConeLog does not collect, store, or share any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone, address, account IDs)
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA, IDFV)
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or HealthKit data
  • Third-party analytics, crash logs, or behavioral telemetry
  • Any data sent to our own servers — ConeLog has no backend

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device using Apple's Core Data framework:

  • Trick library — 100+ preset tricks plus any custom tricks you create
  • Session logs — date, duration, tricks performed, reps, mood, optional notes
  • Environment snapshots (optional) — weather summary, temperature and approximate location, only when you explicitly enable "auto-capture"
  • App preferences — language override, theme, units, default workout settings
  • Subscription state — Pro entitlement flag, validated locally via StoreKit 2 receipts

4. iCloud Sync (Pro Only — Optional)

If you subscribe to ConeLog Pro and enable iCloud sync in Settings, your trick library and session logs are synchronized across your own Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit framework (private database scope).

  • Data is end-to-end encrypted by iCloud and stored only in your personal iCloud account.
  • The developer of ConeLog has no access to the contents of your CloudKit data.
  • You can disable iCloud sync at any time; existing data on the device is preserved.
  • Free-tier users never use CloudKit — no data leaves the device.

6. Third-Party Services

ConeLog integrates with Apple's first-party frameworks only. No third-party SDKs, no analytics platforms, no advertising networks.

  • Apple StoreKit 2 — handles in-app purchases (monthly & yearly Pro subscriptions). Apple's own privacy practices apply.
  • Apple WeatherKit — provides weather data when you enable environment auto-capture.
  • Apple CloudKit — powers iCloud sync for Pro users (private database).

7. In-App Purchases

ConeLog Pro is offered as auto-renewing subscriptions (monthly / yearly). Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We do not see your payment information; we only receive a verified entitlement token via StoreKit 2 to unlock Pro features locally.

8. Data Security

Because all primary data is stored locally on your device, it is protected by iOS device-level encryption when your device is locked with a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID. Pro iCloud sync is protected by Apple's CloudKit encryption.

9. Data Deletion

You can delete ConeLog data at any time:

  • Inside the App: open Settings → Data Management to clear sessions, tricks, or reset to defaults.
  • iCloud (Pro): Settings → iCloud Sync → Disable stops sync; Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage → ConeLog permanently removes server-side copies.
  • System-wide: uninstall the App. iOS will remove all local data associated with ConeLog.

10. Children's Privacy

ConeLog is rated 4+ and is suitable for general audiences. The App does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children under 13. Because no personal data is collected, this policy applies uniformly to all users.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if the App's behavior changes. When that happens, the "Effective Date" above will be updated and a summary of changes will appear in the App Store release notes.
