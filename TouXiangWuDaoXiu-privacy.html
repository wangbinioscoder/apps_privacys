Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-28

Avatar Dance Studio (头像舞蹈秀) is designed as an on-device creative app for making avatar dance videos. We value your privacy and keep core processing local on your device.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not require an account. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, financial information, browsing history, or usage analytics. We do not upload your photos or generated videos to our servers.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Photos selected by you are processed locally to create an avatar badge. Generated preview videos are saved only when you choose to save them to your Photos library.

3. Photo Library Access

  • Photo Library access is used to let you select an avatar image.
  • Add-only Photo Library access is used to save generated avatar dance videos.
  • You may deny these permissions, but related features may not work.

4. Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or cloud storage services.

5. Data Security

Because core processing happens on your device, your selected photos and generated clips remain under your control. Please protect your device with system security features such as passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete generated videos from the Photos app at any time. Deleting the app removes app-local data from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Since no account or server-side collection is used, we do not knowingly collect children's data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features change. The updated version will be posted at this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the app developer through the support contact listed on the App Store product page.
