Privacy Policy

AI Kids Storybook Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

AI Kids Storybook is a parent-facing app that helps families create personalized children’s storybooks with AI-generated stories, illustrations, narration, and PDF export. We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information needed to provide the app’s core features.

Information You Provide

When using the story generation feature, you may provide story prompts such as a child name, age, favorite animal, preferred theme, language option, and bedtime mode. This information is used to generate the requested storybook content.

How Information Is Used

  • To generate personalized story text and illustrations.
  • To provide narration and storybook reading features.
  • To export or print storybooks as PDF files when requested.
  • To manage local app functionality such as generation credits and purchase entitlement state.

AI Processing

Story prompts may be sent to an AI service provider to generate story text, illustrations, or narration. The app does not use this information for advertising tracking, behavioral profiling, or selling personal data.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your full payment card information. The app may store local entitlement or credit information needed to unlock Pro features or story generation credits.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not include third-party advertising SDKs or social networking features. User-provided prompts are used only to provide the requested AI storybook functionality.

Children’s Privacy

The app is intended for parents and guardians to create content for family reading. Parents should avoid entering sensitive personal information. Any child-related prompt information entered by a parent is used only to generate the requested storybook.

Data Retention

Generated storybooks and app preferences may be stored locally on your device. You can delete storybooks from the app or remove app data by deleting the app from your device.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, please contact us at: support@aichildrenstorybook.app

If the support email changes, this page will be updated before the next App Store submission.
