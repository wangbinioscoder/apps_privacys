Help and contact information for AI Kids Storybook.

About the App

AI Kids Storybook helps parents create personalized bedtime storybooks with AI-generated stories, illustrations, bilingual reading, narration, and PDF export.

Common Questions

  • How do I create a story? Enter a child name, age, favorite animal or theme, choose a language mode, and tap the generate button.
  • Can I read in two languages? Yes. The app supports Chinese, English, and bilingual reading modes.
  • How do Pro features work? Pro membership unlocks more AI story generation, cloud narration voices, and premium PDF export.
  • Can I buy credits without subscribing? Yes. The 10-story credit pack provides additional story generations without a subscription.
  • How do I restore purchases? Open the Pro membership page and tap Restore Purchases.
