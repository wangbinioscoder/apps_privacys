About the App

AIStyleAdvisor helps users improve their personal image with AI style scans, smart wardrobe organization, AI try-on, beauty insights, hairstyle and makeup suggestions, 7-day style improvement plans, and full style reports.

Common Questions

  • Why does the app request camera or photo library permission? The app uses photos only when you actively choose or capture images for AI style scans, wardrobe creation, or AI try-on.
  • Why do AI results take time? AI try-on and style analysis may require cloud AI processing. Generation time can vary depending on network and service status.
  • How do I restore my subscription? Open the “Profile” tab, tap “Subscription Management” or “Upgrade Pro”, then tap “Restore Purchases” on the Style Pro page.
  • How can I manage or cancel my subscription? Style Pro is managed by Apple App Store. You can manage or cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings.

关于应用

AI形象顾问（AIStyleAdvisor）帮助用户通过 AI 形象扫描、智能衣橱、AI 试穿、变美建议、发型妆容建议、7 天形象提升计划和完整形象报告来提升个人形象。

常见问题

  • 为什么需要相机或相册权限？应用只会在你主动选择或拍摄图片时，用于生成形象扫描、智能衣橱和 AI 试穿效果。
  • 为什么 AI 结果需要等待？AI 试穿和形象分析可能需要调用云端 AI 服务处理图片，生成时间会受网络和服务状态影响。
  • 如何恢复订阅？进入“我的”Tab，点击“订阅管理”或“升级 Pro”，在 Style Pro 页面点击“恢复购买”。
  • 如何管理或取消订阅？Style Pro 为自动续期订阅，由 Apple App Store 管理。你可以在 Apple ID 的订阅设置中随时管理或取消。
