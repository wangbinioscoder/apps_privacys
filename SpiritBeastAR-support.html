Spirit Beast AR Support

Support page for Spirit Beast AR / 灵镜神兽园

About the App

Spirit Beast AR is an augmented reality creature collection app. Scan everyday objects, summon 3D spirit beasts into your real surroundings, capture them, and complete your catalog.

Getting Started

  • Allow camera access when prompted so the app can display AR content.
  • Point the camera at books, plants, toys, cups, computers, or metal objects.
  • Hold steady while the Mirror scans the scene.
  • When a beast appears, follow the on-screen capture prompt.
  • Captured beasts can be viewed in the catalog and summoned again in AR photo mode.

Camera Permission

The camera is required for AR display and on-device object scanning. Camera frames are not uploaded by the app. If AR does not start, open iOS Settings, find Spirit Beast AR, and enable Camera permission.

Frequently Asked Questions

The beast does not appear.
Move to a brighter area, point at a clear object, and keep the phone steady for a moment.

AR tracking feels unstable.
Use a well-lit space with visible surfaces. Avoid blank walls, mirrors, or very dark rooms.

My progress disappeared.
Progress is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app can remove local catalog and achievement data.

Can I use the app without GPS?
Yes. Spirit Beast AR is designed for indoor object scanning and does not require GPS exploration.

Privacy Policy

The app is designed to work locally and does not collect personal identity information. Please refer to the privacy policy page provided with the App Store listing.

Contact

If you need support, please contact the developer through the App Store support channel and include your device model, iOS version, and a brief description of the issue.
