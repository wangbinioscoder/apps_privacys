Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

1. Introduction

LandlordLedger ("we", "our", or "the App") is a local offline rent management tool designed for individual landlords. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Key Point: LandlordLedger does NOT collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All your data remains exclusively on your device.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be completely transparent: LandlordLedger does NOT collect any of the following:

  • Personal identification information
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Financial information
  • Contact information
  • Device identifiers
  • Cookies or similar tracking technologies

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device using Core Data (Apple's local database framework):

  • Property information (address, rent amount, contract dates)
  • Tenant information (name, phone number, move-in date)
  • Rent payment records
  • Utility meter readings and calculations
  • Reminder schedules

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it using the backup feature.

4. Third-Party Services

LandlordLedger does NOT use any third-party services, SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising networks. The App operates entirely offline without any network connections.

5. Data Security

Your data is protected by:

  • Apple's iOS device-level encryption
  • App sandbox isolation (iOS security model)
  • No network transmission (data cannot be intercepted)
  • Optional backup/export under your full control

6. Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

  • Delete individual records within the App at any time
  • Uninstalling the App will permanently delete all stored data
  • No residual data exists on any server after deletion

7. Children's Privacy

LandlordLedger is not directed at children under 13. The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
