Avatar Dance Studio Support

Support page for 头像舞蹈秀

Avatar badge dance video app

Avatar Dance Studio helps you choose a character, add an avatar badge, preview dance actions, and record short dance clips on your device.

How to Use

  • Select a dance character from the role list.
  • Preview actions and switch categories to find a preferred movement.
  • Upload an avatar image to create a stage avatar badge.
  • Choose whether the badge stays fixed or follows the character's face motion.
  • Record and save the final clip when you are satisfied.

Photo and Video Permissions

The app uses Photo Library access only when you choose an avatar image or save a generated clip. Avatar processing happens locally on your device.

Troubleshooting

  • If an avatar image does not appear, choose another clear portrait image and try again.
  • If preview performance is low, close other apps and reopen Avatar Dance Studio.
  • If saving fails, check that Photos permission is enabled in iOS Settings.
